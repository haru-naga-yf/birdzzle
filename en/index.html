Solve more, collect more birds.

Exactly one bird in every row, column and territory. Solve real Star Battle / Queens / Two Not Touch style logic puzzles, welcome birds, raise them and complete your bird guide.

Download on the App StoreFree. Plays fully offline.Already installed? Open in the app

Zero guessing

Every puzzle has a unique solution and is verified to be solvable by logic alone.

Raise birds

Spend the coins you earn to welcome eggs and birds, pair them up and breed new species. 48 species in all.

Easy to play

No stamina or play limits. Unlimited free Undo/Redo. Fully offline. 13 languages.

Rules

  • Exactly one bird in each row
  • Exactly one bird in each column
  • Exactly one bird in each territory (colored region)
  • Birds can never touch, even diagonally
