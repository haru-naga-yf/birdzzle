Welcome, raise and breed birds in your aviary

Spend the coins you earn from puzzles to welcome eggs and birds, feed them and watch them grow. Grown birds sometimes bring back a puzzle hint.

  • No gacha: birds come from the shop, clear rewards and breeding
  • Pair them up to breed, and give them names
  • Every probability is public in the in-app list

NEWA daily Bird Contest

Compete every day with the voice, coat and wits of the birds you raised. You are ranked within a block against other players' birds (and sometimes dummies), asynchronously.

  • Train each bird's voice, coat and wits with dedicated items
  • Affection rises when you keep tapping the cage during aviary events (it does not grow while you are away)
  • Rewards are fixed amounts of in-game coins and items, unrelated to real money

Complete the 48-species bird guide

Only a few birds are sold in the shop. The rest are discovered by pairing birds. “Reachable”, “One step away” and “Auto pair” help you find the next new species.

  • Discovered species are recorded in the guide
  • Hints for undiscovered species appear as “maybe…?”
  • Tidy up with “Sell in bulk”
