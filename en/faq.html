FAQ

Do any puzzles require guessing?

No. Every bundled puzzle has a unique solution and is verified by our solver to be solvable by logic alone.

Is there a stamina system or play limit?

No. Play any number of puzzles at any time. Undo and Redo are unlimited and free.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Both the puzzles and the bird raising work without a network connection.

Are there ads?

A banner ad appears at the bottom of some screens. Full-screen ads only appear after you clear a puzzle, with a minimum interval (and not during your first 20 clears). Full-screen ads never appear while you are playing a board.

Are there in-app purchases?

The current version has no in-app purchases.

Do I get birds from gacha / random draws?

No. Birds come from the shop, puzzle clear rewards and breeding. The probabilities for breeding, hint payouts and birds losing their spirits are public in the in-app Probability screen and in this site's changelog.

Do more birds cost me?

Adult birds eat feed. If feed stays at zero an adult bird may lose its spirits (no eggs or hints, and it cannot enter the contest) until you give it medicine from the shop. Earning coins from puzzles and buying feed keeps them healthy. The Probability screen shows the numbers.

Supported devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad (as an iPhone app) on iOS 17 or later. 13 languages: Japanese, English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Korean, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Russian, Turkish and Indonesian.

Can I move my data to a new device?

Yes, via iPhone backup / Quick Start the whole app moves over. Cross-device account sync is not available yet.

Where do I report bugs or requests?

Please use the support page.
