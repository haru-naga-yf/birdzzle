Birdzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Birdzzle is a puzzle and bird-raising game. The core game (puzzles and your aviary) works without signing in and without an internet connection. Only when you use the optional online features — publishing your aviary, friends, the bird contest, and account linking / cloud backup — does the app send information to a server (Google LLC's Firebase), as described below.

Information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, clear history, statistics, coins, birds and settings are stored only on your device. We do not sync with iCloud. When you change phones, the app may move with an iCloud backup or Quick Start (an Apple feature).

Information handled by the online features

Only if you use the following features, information is stored in Google LLC's Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore; data location: Tokyo, Japan). While you don't use them, no bird data is sent to the server.

  • Anonymous user ID: a random identifier issued by Firebase Authentication when you first use an online feature. We do not collect your name or email address.
  • Public aviary and friends: your account name (shown to other users), friend code, friendships, identifiers of users you blocked, and — if you set visibility to "Friends only" or "Everyone" — your aviary contents (bird species, growth stage, birthday, asking price) and last-active time. Bird names and pairing information are not included. While visibility is "Private", no bird data is stored.
  • Bird contest: the entered bird's data (anonymous user ID, the bird's name if you named it, species, voice / plumage / wisdom / affection values, contest level, appeal results). It is shown to other entrants of the same hour on the result screen, and entries are deleted after about two days.
  • Account linking and cloud backup (optional): you can link your anonymous account with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Apple does not provide your name or email to us. If you sign in with Google, your Google account's email address is recorded in Firebase Authentication for sign-in purposes (it is not shown to other users). Only for linked accounts, your game data (coins, birds, progress) is compressed and backed up to the server so you can restore it on a new device.
  • Reports: when you report another user, your identifier, the target, the reason and a note (up to 200 characters).

Purposes of use

Providing the public aviary, friends, bird contest and backup restore; handling abuse (reports, blocking, suspension); keeping the service safe; and cleaning up abandoned accounts.

Provision to third parties / transfer abroad

We entrust storing and processing the above information to Firebase by Google LLC (United States). We do not provide it to any other third party except as required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • From "Delete account" in the in-app Account and Backup screen you can delete your data on the server at any time (name, friend code, public aviary, friendships, block list, backup and anonymous user ID).
  • Accounts that have not been used for a long time (guideline: 365 days) may be deleted by the operator. Public aviaries may be deleted after 180 days of inactivity.
  • Report records are kept for up to one year after review to confirm the content and prevent recurrence.

Advertising

This app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may use information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to deliver ads.

  • We ask for tracking permission through iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If you decline, non-personalized ads are shown and every feature of the app remains available.
  • Users in the EU/UK and other applicable regions see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and ad delivery follows your choice.
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Usage analytics

To improve the app we use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymous usage data such as puzzle clears and feature usage. This does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Website analytics

On this official website we use Google Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymous access data such as pages viewed and clicks on the "App Store" and "Open in app" buttons. Analytics cookies are used only if you choose "Agree" on the banner (the site works the same if you decline). Advertising features (such as ad personalization) are disabled. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be withdrawn by clearing the site data. Website analytics are aggregated in the same Google Analytics property as the app's usage analytics, but we do not link a website visitor with an app user as individuals (only aggregate information such as "which page the app was opened from").

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Requests and contact

For requests to disclose, correct or delete information we hold, or any questions about this policy, please contact us from the support page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change without prior notice. For important changes we will announce them on this page and in the app. The Terms of Use for the online features are here.
